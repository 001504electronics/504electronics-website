How we grade condition

The grade describes the outside only. Every Mac passes the same battery, storage, display and port tests.

New

Sealed in the box

Unopened and never used.

A

Like new

No visible marks at arm's length. Battery above 90% of original capacity.

B

Light wear

Small scuffs or hairline marks on the case. Screen is clean. Battery above 85%.

C

Visible wear

Dents or scratches you will notice. Works like the others, priced lower.

Questions buyers ask

Are refurbished Macs reliable?

Each one is tested on battery health, storage, memory, display, keyboard, speakers, ports and wireless before it is listed. Anything that fails is repaired with working parts or removed.

What comes with my Mac?

The Mac, a power adapter and cable, and a clean install of the current macOS it supports. Refurbished units ship in plain packaging.

Do you ship outside the country?

Not yet. We ship within the United States with tracked, insured delivery. Orders placed before 2 pm leave the next business day.
